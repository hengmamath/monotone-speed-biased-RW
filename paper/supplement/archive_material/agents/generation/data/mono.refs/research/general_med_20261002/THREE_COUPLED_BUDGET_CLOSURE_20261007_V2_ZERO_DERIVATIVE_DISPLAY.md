# Endpoint derivative clarification after final audit

2026-10-07. Addendum only; audited original proof and source preserved.

For f(x)=1-25[x^2-(3/4)x^3-4x^4],

    f'(x)=x[-50+(225/4)x+400x^2].

The bracket increases on x>=0 and is negative at 1/4. Consequently
f'(x)<0 for 0<x<=1/4, whereas f'(0)=0. Thus f strictly decreases
on [0,1/4] and f(x)>=f(1/4)=31/256 there. The conditional closure
and all its constants are unchanged. The three general budgets (J)
remain unproved.
